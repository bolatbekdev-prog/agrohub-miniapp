# Design notes — AGROHUB Atelier

**Intent:** A prospective farmer should recognize agriculture immediately, understand the value proposition within seconds, and know what to click next.

**Photography first:** A farm scene fills the hero; service cards show field equipment, temperature-controlled storage, and produce export. Images are locally optimized `.webp` files rather than live remote image dependencies.

**Quiet palette:** Deep forest green signals agriculture and stability. Off-white, muted sage and pale lime provide contrast without fluorescent accents. A restrained glass surface is used only where it adds functional hierarchy.

**Typography:** Manrope with system fallback. Moderate weights, generous line height and consistent letter spacing keep the interface legible in Uzbek, Russian and English.

**Shapes:** Two soft, semi-transparent glass circles in the pinned section react to scroll position. There are no large blurry ornaments covering headlines or buttons.

**Motion:** One intentional hero parallax plus a cinematic sticky four-step explanation. Entrances are subtle and triggered once. No forced scroll snapping or sudden jumping.

**UX principles:** Product-first language, clear primary buttons, modest card count, truthful demo labeling and form feedback. Unlike a showcase-only landing page, search, filters and commission calculation work.

**Responsive:** Four-column service strip collapses, imagery crops naturally, service cards become single-column, mobile navigation turns into a real toggle, and sticky storytelling becomes image-first on phones.
